### 31.18. DEV-18 — Product value and differentiation before architecture freeze

**Type:** ECOSYSTEM POLICY | **Origin:** USER POLICY | **Applies:** idea refinement and planning of substantial applications

Before architecture freeze, the product MUST answer: **If someone else can create software performing the same general function, why should someone use this application?** Record actual value such as workflow/usability, privacy/local operation/user ownership, reliability/portability/accessibility, performance/interoperability, specialist fit, low friction, execution quality, longevity/recovery/trust, or precise fit to an individual owner's needs. No fake commercial moat, novelty for its own sake, or market claim is required for private/internal products.

The answer MUST influence relevant workflows, feature priorities, UX/UI, architecture, privacy, platform support, integrations, performance and offline/local behavior; an interchangeable implementation with only a slogan is insufficient. Record what should not be built alongside purpose and alternatives under [DEV-2](101-%20Understanding%20the%20User%27s%20Idea.md#dev-2), [DEV-3](../2-%20Researching%20Existing%20Projects%20and%20Filling%20the%20Gaps/203-%20Researching%20Existing%20GitHub%20Projects.md#dev-3), and [DEV-7](../6-%20Writing%20the%20Complete%20Master%20Plan/601-%20Defining%20All%20Project%20Requirements.md#dev-7). Verify the connection in applicable phase/UI review under [DEV-13](../14-%20Reviewing%20the%20Entire%20Application/1408-%20Checking%20Rule%20and%20Requirement%20Compliance.md#dev-13) and [UI-7](../5-%20Designing%20the%20UI%20and%20UX/510-%20Reviewing%20and%20Approving%20the%20Design.md#ui-7).
