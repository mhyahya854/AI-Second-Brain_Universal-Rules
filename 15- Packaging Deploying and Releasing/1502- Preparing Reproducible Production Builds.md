### 25.5. SEC-5 — Release reconstruction and critical-dependency continuity

**Type:** ECOSYSTEM POLICY | **Origin:** USER POLICY | **Applies:** software built/distributed as executables, and dependencies whose disappearance materially threatens building, operation, migration, recovery, or maintenance

Projects producing executable software MUST preserve durable instructions and inputs sufficient to reconstruct a supported release or equivalent functioning build where reasonably practicable. Record applicable source revision, toolchain/compiler/runtime requirements, build commands, dependency resolution/locks, required build-time assets and generated-code steps, platform prerequisites, packaging/signing procedures, release provenance, and known limits. A future maintainer MUST NOT need undocumented tribal knowledge to determine how a supported release is produced. A tiny utility may satisfy this with concise prerequisites, its source revision, and one deterministic build command; larger distributions need proportionate detail and evidence under [TEST-1](../13-%20Testing%20Debugging%20and%20Repairing/1301-%20Preparing%20the%20Complete%20Test%20Environment.md#test-1).

For critical frameworks, runtimes, SDKs, toolchains, package ecosystems, libraries, required local services, model runtimes/providers, APIs, or platform components, the App Plan MUST record a proportionate disappearance-risk disposition where material. Options include replacement paths, interface boundaries, preserved source/vendor snapshots, migration/archive strategies, supported alternatives, or deliberately accepted dependency risk with applicable authority. Match treatment to criticality, replaceability, maintenance burden, license, security, and intended lifetime. Existing selection and foundation provenance follow [SEC-3](../3-%20Deciding%20the%20Stack/307-%20Choosing%20Libraries%20and%20Dependencies.md#sec-3) and [SEC-4](../2-%20Researching%20Existing%20Projects%20and%20Filling%20the%20Gaps/209-%20Choosing%20What%20to%20Reuse%20or%20Build.md#sec-4).

State material reconstruction limits honestly, including proprietary SDKs, unavailable signing systems, hardware, closed platforms, discontinued services, and legal restrictions where relevant. No perpetual rebuild guarantee, bit-for-bit reproducibility, mandatory SBOM format, universal vendoring, permanent toolchain-binary retention, or archive of every historical release is required. Preservation does not require continued use of vulnerable obsolete components; distinguish preserved reference material from safe supported execution. No mandatory cloud build infrastructure is introduced. Protect signing secrets and respect access/retention constraints under [SEC-2](../9-%20Preparing%20the%20Development%20Environment/908-%20Establishing%20Permissions%20and%20Secrets.md#sec-2) and [PRIV-1](../6-%20Writing%20the%20Complete%20Master%20Plan/606-%20Documenting%20Data%20Security%20and%20Recovery.md#priv-1).
