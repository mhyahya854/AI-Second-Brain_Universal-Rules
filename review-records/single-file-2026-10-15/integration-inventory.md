## 3. Historical records versus continuing operational requirements

### 3.1 Active material that must be embedded in the single file

The governing rules are all 113 frozen rule bodies with their classifications, origins and applicability, the classification/definition tables, 33 Skill-family responsibilities, and applicable tables such as NAS modes, platforms, UI review signals, and A–P lenses. Mechanism examples remain examples; their qualifiers matter.

The separate Green/Red protocol adds operational detail already subordinate to GOV-3/4/5. Consolidate the substance, not the obsolete instruction to create a separate file:

1. Recognize exact invocations `GREEN FLAG`, `RED FLAG`, `GREEN FIX` without requiring the huge prior prompt or chat.
2. Establish actual branch, HEAD, tree/status, normative text and task authority before each run; do not trust summaries, counts, prior author claims or favorable reviews.
3. Green researches sources statically, records authority/revision/license/scope/adoption and honest inaccessible-source status, integrates authorized owner policy, resolves contradictions and validates. It does not execute downloaded guidance merely to research it, freeze by inference, or grant ratification authority.
4. Before Red, verify explicit owner freeze/delegation tied to the exact candidate and permitted repair/review scope. If absent, use `RED FLAG REQUIRES OWNER-AUTHORIZED PLAN FREEZE`. No self-authorized freeze marker or skill invocation supplies authority. Current owner mission already delegates the relevant integration/review workflow; obsolete past gates must not manufacture another permission request.
5. Freeze identifies source revision, tested normative bytes/digest, status and authority. A commit cannot contain its own hash; use an immutable later receipt or content-digest/freeze-record relationship and distinguish repository HEAD from the review target.
6. Freeze closes ordinary brainstorming, not defect correction or explicit owner-authorized reopening. Post-freeze changes need validated findings, demonstrated contradiction/falsehood/unsafe ambiguity/material incompleteness, or explicit reopening. Findings established outside a completed Red round need traceable records and applicable repair authority, not invented prior review findings.
7. Red attacks written frozen text read-only: no normative edits, fixes, repair commits, or charitable reinterpretation. A non-normative saved report needs actual task authorization and cannot mutate review-target bytes. Historical evidence explains provenance but never repairs missing wording.
8. Use independently tasked reviewers when supported, otherwise distinct sequential passes and disclose limits. Preserve actual reviewer/session identity, visible model facts, configured versus unverified deployed identities, shared context/environment, prior finding exposure, and whether reviewer authored/repaired target. Never fabricate external audit or model diversity.
9. Exercise all A–P lenses, including both tiny-tool bureaucracy and large-lifetime loopholes, plus new-owner conflicts. The existing table is an active attack-scope requirement, not merely history.
10. Classify every finding BLOCKER, MATERIAL, NON-BLOCKING or NOT AN ISSUE, preserving severity definitions. Each substantiated finding needs exact rule/section/wording, scenario, why existing rules fail, severity/confidence, smallest repair direction, regression risk. Rejected hypotheses need explicit disposition; stylistic taste alone is not defect.
11. Each run reports observed revision/tree/scope/evidence/findings/dispositions/blockers/next authoritative action. Distinguish tests actually run from assumptions or inherited reports.
12. Green Fix checks exact report and affected revision, repairs validated findings only within delegation, preserves owner policy, records every finding's disposition, validates regressions, commits locally and returns new exact HEAD. It cannot introduce unrelated policy under repair label, rewrite prior findings, or ratify.
13. Every normative repair invalidates previous clean-round credit. Reidentify exact bytes and covered freeze/repair/review authority; new rounds review the changed candidate, not a remembered earlier state.
14. Ratification recommendation requires zero outstanding BLOCKER/MATERIAL findings, explicit prior dispositions, no old material regression, current structural/factuality/proportionality checks, and two consecutive independent clean Red rounds after the last normative repair. Merely two reports from the same author does not establish independence; explicitly report unavailable independence.
15. Every Red outcome discloses limits. Only zero BLOCKER/MATERIAL may use the required sentence: **No material defect was identified under the defined review battery. This does not prove absolute correctness, security, completeness, or immunity to future unknown conditions.** A failing report must not assert that clean outcome and must say more defects may remain. Never call the constitution unbeatable.
16. Ratification is a separate owner decision identifying exact revision and applicability date. Publication, merge, tags/releases and adoption of governed apps have distinct authority. Current publication delegation does not automatically apply to future amendments or other repositories.
17. Future amendments record version, rationale, affected IDs, source decisions/evidence, prior and replacement text scope, new candidate status, exact freeze/review identities and review invalidation. Preserve historical findings rather than modifying them to fit revisions. No implicit governed-app migration.

## 4. Conflict resolutions and regression traps

1. **No fallbacks versus safety/recovery.** Define the forbidden act as replacing the approved capability's implementation upon failure or presenting substitute behavior/results as its success. Same-operation replay with safety, rollback, recovery, restore, containment and truthful refusal remain valid. A report must distinguish unavailable/partial/unknown results. Independent local features continuing during scanner/Hermes/network failure are not substitute implementations of the failed capability.
2. **No fallbacks versus planned platforms/topology.** Explicit platform adapters, deliberately selected architectures, data migration, and declared peer/NAS topology transitions may be normal approved behavior. They cannot become a broad 'approved fallback' exception allowing runtime provider/DB/algorithm replacement. Distinguish planned composition/transition from failure-triggered alternative execution.
3. **No fallbacks versus SEC-6.** Existing alternative/supplementary scanner permission means design-time selection or approved multi-engine composition/explicit later migration, not selecting a second scanner after first failure to obtain CLEAN. Required scan failures stay gates. Never relabel unscanned content clean to preserve offline convenience.
4. **App-independent data versus mobile deletion.** A private container alone cannot survive OS deletion; independent representation must exist before the destructive lifecycle event. Plan storage-provider/external export/backup/key dependencies and accepted recovery point, with real disappearance recovery evidence. App uninstall hook or promised future export isn't sufficient. Retention, consent, authorized erasure and feasible platform APIs constrain the contract.
5. **Data survival versus derived/disposable state.** Preserve authoritative and retained meaning, including irreplaceable generated outputs; not all caches need backup. Keep original/derivative, authority, reproduction target, ownership, sensitivity and retention independent. The strengthened rule must not demand every transient byte forever.
6. **Open-source-first versus legal rights.** Verify exact license at pin, bundled/transitive notices and modification/distribution duties. GitHub visibility/no license is not permission. Do not invent that separate-process integration guarantees GPL compliance or that all open-source dependencies force publication of private app code.
7. **ZIP reuse versus provenance and maintenance.** Prefer pinned ZIP, but archive bytes may change packaging independent of source tree; record exact origin, revision and digest of acquired bytes. Keep attribution/local differences/update responsibility. Static inspection and safe extraction precede execution; user-supplied ZIPs are not automatically trusted.
8. **Rust-first versus Tauri/frontend.** Rust domain core remains shared; webview HTML/CSS/assets and authored executable frontend have different roles. Use actual component-level rationale/delegation for non-Rust logic and do not duplicate core invariants. Tauri preference cannot force unsuitable accessibility/platform design.
9. **Shared core versus feature-first.** One shared logical product core can consist of cohesive feature modules. Don't introduce a giant universal backend or duplicate domain logic per UI/device. Filesystem symmetry is not architecture.
10. **Broad docs versus small context.** One comprehensive governance document needs stable anchors, navigation, compact entry/state/routing and bounded relevant extracts. Future app docs can be many files with one intended-spec authority. Do not require a 4B model to ingest every document or 33 Skills simultaneously; do require complete applicable execution contract and safety boundaries.
11. **Comments versus drift and third-party source.** Meaningful authored/modified source requires beginner-friendly native docs; generated/vendored files may need adjacent/generated documentation preserving exact upstream bytes and provenance. If using this practical qualification, state it explicitly and preserve requested discoverability. Update headers/maps in same change, verify actual edges, record dynamic relations and avoid false dependencies.
12. **Strict order versus repairs/emergencies.** Ordinary next subphase waits for current completion. Scope-contained repair is part of current work; new dependency requires authorized replan; material emergency uses DEV-17 actual authority/evidence/reconciliation and then returns to ordered execution. No arbitrary out-of-order feature jump or permanent bypass.
13. **Completion versus exception.** ✅ means applicable acceptance criteria genuinely passed or specifically authorized exception is truthfully explicit; unrun required check is never pass. Parent cannot be done merely because individual headings have marks. Preserve original contract/record and reopened regressions accurately.
14. **Visual alternatives versus predictable accessibility.** Three concepts exercise real representative workflows after requirements, not three visual skins. A+B can be simultaneous, C only explicit request. Reuse motion infrastructure while respecting reduced motion and non-motion meaning. Notion palette is preference; do not remove old semantic/status/accessibility color precedence or 60/30/10's optional heuristic qualification.
15. **Upstream instructions versus authority.** Preserve original owner-supplied Skill instructions for personal use without executing conflicting rules or mutating them to appear compliant. External wrapper/review disposition records conflicts and effective constrained authority. No hidden dynamic remote instruction updates mid-phase.
16. **One file versus future app blueprint.** Update ARCH-1's old 'No fixed folders' phrasing to mean no universally rigid physical tree beyond new declared substantial-app default; future app tree includes AGENTS/README/docs/code, governance repo remains exactly MASTER_PLAN.md. Preserve tiny-app proportionality, no empty folders, framework-driven adaptations.
17. **Current authority versus stale freeze gates.** GOV-4 literal old 'There is nothing else left to add' wait, DEV-5 statement constitution review 'remains blocked', SKILL-2's old final-attack limitation, header/section30 and external protocol checkpoints are historical after owner's explicit reopening/execute mission. Replace with a general authority rule plus current mission evidence. Do not merely add new authority text while retaining contradictory active prohibitions.
18. **New review versus historical convergence.** The a9c597c clean rounds and even b5ac54f audit are evidence about old bytes only. New normative repairs reset both rounds. Metadata changes after reviews need precise distinction from normative changes and exact verified artifact identity; safest publish the exact reviewed bytes and put receipts in history refs/final response.
19. **Platform selection versus old first-class baselines.** Owner now permits app-specific narrower subsets; preserve old named first-class baselines within desktop commitment and route genuine exclusions through reasoned scope/exception authority. Do not silently downgrade all four to optional examples.
20. **Project State versus simple Markdown.** New `project-state.md` satisfies both human and machine use only if its structure is deterministic enough for parsing (e.g. documented fields/embedded structured block). The old mandatory machine-readable obligation must not disappear behind prose-only guidance. Record last observed revision rather than self-referential new commit.
