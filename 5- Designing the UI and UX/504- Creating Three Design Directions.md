## 38. Visual concepts, interaction modes, and named design guidance

*Section 38 rules across lifecycle files: [DESIGN-1](#design-1), [DESIGN-2](508-%20Designing%20States%20Interactions%20and%20Motion.md#design-2), [DESIGN-3](502-%20Researching%20Design%20References.md#design-3).*

<a id="design-1"></a>
### 38.1. DESIGN-1 — Three rendered concepts and deliberate direction

**Type:** DEFAULT | **Origin:** USER POLICY | **Applies:** substantial new user interfaces and material visual redesign

After understanding actual features and workflows, the design process SHOULD produce **three genuinely different rendered concepts** for representative real application workflows. Superficial theme variations, empty moodboards, and generic dashboards do not satisfy the concept purpose. Each direction SHOULD make its information hierarchy, typography, layout, identity, interaction assumptions, and meaningful states assessable. The owner may choose, combine, refine, or delegate selection. When selection is delegated, the AI MAY compare alternatives internally and present the strongest justified direction, recording the decision and why it fits.

Prioritize refined typography, intentional negative space, elegant layout, strong hierarchy, purposeful motion, accessible interactions, thoughtful identity, responsive adaptation, and polished real states. Airy, calm design and the full Notion color palette are owner preference references, not compulsory themes or claims of license to copy proprietary work. Applications MUST NOT be forced into identical fonts, colors, cards, navigation, or design language. Aesthetic formulas, generic generated layouts, arbitrary anti-card bans, and compulsory decorative imagery MUST NOT replace product-specific reasoning. Measurable accessibility standards and justified technical budgets remain applicable; “no aesthetic formulas” does not prohibit contrast, target-size, or performance acceptance criteria. Apply [UI-4](501-%20Understanding%20the%20Desired%20Experience.md#ui-4), [UI-5](506-%20Building%20the%20Design%20System.md#ui-5), [UI-6](../11-%20Building%20the%20Frontend/1106-%20Implementing%20Loading%20Empty%20and%20Error%20States.md#ui-6), [UI-7](510-%20Reviewing%20and%20Approving%20the%20Design.md#ui-7), including actual rendered review.
