These are *favorites for consideration*, not requirements to use every site, replicate a particular appearance, download assets, install software, or adopt an identical stack.

Lexington Themes is described by its current public documentation as using **Astro and Tailwind CSS**. The owner **may purchase properly licensed themes and adapt them**. A licensed theme does not transfer unrestricted redistribution or marketplace rights. Verify the actual purchased license, provenance, asset rights, compatible versions, and conversion limits before reuse, including any reuse outside the original ecosystem. Existing [DESIGN-3](#design-3), [ASSET-2](../11-%20Building%20the%20Frontend/1101-%20Implementing%20Design%20System%20Foundations.md#asset-2), and [SEC-3](../3-%20Deciding%20the%20Stack/307-%20Choosing%20Libraries%20and%20Dependencies.md#sec-3) remain authoritative.

Do not infer a blanket adoption of Astro/Tailwind/Figma or impose a mandatory implementation workflow.
