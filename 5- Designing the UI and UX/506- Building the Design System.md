### 6.9. UI-5 — Coherent design system and purposeful presentation

**Type:** ECOSYSTEM POLICY | **Origin:** USER POLICY | **Applies:** substantial user-facing applications and supported themes/platform presentations

Applications MUST define a reusable design system or equivalent coherent design contract for relevant typography, sizes/weights/line heights, spacing/grid/rhythm, colors/surfaces, borders/radii, elevation/shadows, icons, motion, focus, interaction states, and responsive behavior. Do not improvise these independently per screen. Semantic design tokens SHOULD represent reusable visual constants where practical; names such as `surface.primary`, `text.muted`, `action.primary`, `status.error`, `space.1`, or `motion.quick` are examples, not universal naming. Equivalent controls SHOULD share semantic behavior for primary/destructive actions, modal dismissal, validation, search, selection, navigation, and notifications instead of arbitrary conventions for novelty.

Color balance MUST follow the application's actual hierarchy, content, semantic states, and accessibility needs rather than a prescribed aesthetic ratio or formula. Deliberately identify base/supporting surfaces and emphasis where useful. Semantic error/danger/warning/success/information/focus/selection communication and accessibility MUST take precedence over decorative balance. Primary accent SHOULD retain meaning rather than indiscriminately color every icon, heading, card, border, and control. The product-specific direction and owner preferences follow [DESIGN-1](504-%20Creating%20Three%20Design%20Directions.md#design-1).

Supported dark mode MUST be deliberately designed rather than blindly invert light mode: define relevant surfaces, hierarchy, contrast, text tones, borders, shadows/elevation, focus/semantic states, and image treatment. Themes retain product identity without requiring identical visual treatment. Typography SHOULD be deliberately selected for readability, hierarchy, platform, supported languages, weights, performance, licensing, and longevity; Inter, Roboto, or framework defaults are valid when reasoned. Iconography SHOULD use a coherent family/style with deliberate reasons for mixed strokes, filled/outline styles, or generated imagery; vector artwork remains subject to [ASSET-1](../11-%20Building%20the%20Frontend/1101-%20Implementing%20Design%20System%20Foundations.md#asset-1).

Density SHOULD reflect task frequency, information complexity, expertise, device size, and accessibility, rather than universally impose huge cards, spacing, or typography on finance, developer, media, medical, settings, and other products. Motion SHOULD communicate transition, relationship, hierarchy, continuity, progress, or feedback; respect reduced-motion needs under [UI-3](509-%20Applying%20Accessibility%20and%20Localization.md#ui-3). Visual decoration MUST NOT obscure hierarchy, navigation, actions, application state, errors/warnings, permissions, security/sync state, or progress. Product identity MUST respect applicable navigation, keyboard/windowing/gesture/back/menu/input/selection/system-control conventions; cross-platform consistency means shared identity and semantics, not identical pixels. [UI-2](507-%20Designing%20Screens%20and%20Responsive%20Layouts.md#ui-2) and [CORE-1](../4-%20Planning%20the%20Architecture%20and%20Data/403-%20Designing%20the%20Shared%20Core.md#core-1) still govern adaptation and common product truth.

Establish the core design system before proliferating independently designed screens. Avoid artificial sparsity and chaotic density through grouping/hierarchy. Icon aggregators do not license every underlying asset; verify collection/asset licensing and provenance under [ASSET-2](../11-%20Building%20the%20Frontend/1101-%20Implementing%20Design%20System%20Foundations.md#asset-2) and [SEC-3](../3-%20Deciding%20the%20Stack/307-%20Choosing%20Libraries%20and%20Dependencies.md#sec-3). Motion MUST NOT be the only channel of important meaning. Refine hover/pressed/focus/loading/transition/scroll/selection/drag/success/error micro-interactions after the interaction structure is sound; polish does not rescue missing information architecture.
