# PART XXI — RED FLAG FINDING CLASSIFICATION

Every finding MUST be classified:

## BLOCKER

Unsafe to ratify.

Could materially create:

- data loss;
- security failure;
- fundamental contradiction;
- unenforceable core architecture;
- dangerous falsehood.

## MATERIAL

Must be fixed before ratification but not immediately catastrophic.

## NON-BLOCKING

Improvement worth recording but not sufficient to prevent candidate progress.

## NOT AN ISSUE

Attack hypothesis rejected after examination.

Do not inflate stylistic preferences into defects.

---

# 136. RED FLAG EVIDENCE

Each finding needs:

- exact rule/section;
- exact problematic wording;
- attack scenario;
- why existing rules do not already resolve it;
- severity;
- confidence;
- smallest viable repair direction;
- possible regression risk.

Red Flag does NOT apply the repair.

# PART XXIII — CONVERGENCE

The process may recommend ratification only when:

1. zero BLOCKER findings remain;
2. zero MATERIAL findings remain;
3. all prior blocker/material findings have explicit dispositions;
4. no old defect materially regressed;
5. current candidate passes structural validation;
6. current candidate passes technical factuality review;
7. current candidate passes proportionality review;
8. two consecutive independent Red Flag rounds after the last normative repair produce no BLOCKER or MATERIAL findings.

If true independent reviewers are unavailable:

state that limitation explicitly.

Do not fake independence.

---

# 138. RESIDUAL RISK

Every Red report must disclose that review does not prove absolute correctness, security, completeness, or immunity to future unknown conditions.

Only when the review identifies zero BLOCKER and zero MATERIAL findings, state:

> No material defect was identified under the defined review battery. This does not prove absolute correctness, security, completeness, or immunity to future unknown conditions.

If any BLOCKER or MATERIAL finding is identified, do not assert the clean-result sentence as the outcome. Report the material findings and state that other defects may still exist and the attack does not prove all defects were discovered. This applies before or after any convergence assessment.

Do NOT call the constitution “unbeatable.”
