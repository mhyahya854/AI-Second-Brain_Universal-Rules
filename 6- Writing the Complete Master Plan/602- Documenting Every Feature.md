### 39.2. BLUEPRINT-2 — One complete authoritative App Master Plan

**Type:** ECOSYSTEM POLICY | **Origin:** USER POLICY | **Applies:** application specification and related technical documentation

`Master Plan/Master Plan/master plan.md`, or its explicitly mapped equivalent, MUST contain the complete authoritative intended application specification, with extensive meaningful hierarchical numbered headings. Supporting documents supply evidence, detailed technical explanations, and execution records; they MUST NOT become competing sources of intended product behavior. Use direct explanations and precise links for detail while ensuring the Master Plan states every material product decision and governing contract. No arbitrary heading cap is permitted, and empty/redundant headings MUST NOT inflate apparent completeness.

Coverage MUST include, where applicable: vision/purpose; users/problems; success criteria; differentiation; research; GitHub comparisons/reuse; requirements/assumptions; complete feature inventory and individual feature specifications; workflows/navigation/interactions; screens and states; platforms/accessibility; identity/design system/motion; architecture/boundaries; technology; structures/storage; import/export; offline/NAS/sync; security/privacy; AI capabilities/permissions; failure/no-fallback behavior; data survival/recovery; performance/testing/integration; deployment/updates/migrations/maintenance; risks/rejected alternatives; development phases; and acceptance/evidence. The additional contracts already required by [DEV-7](601-%20Defining%20All%20Project%20Requirements.md#dev-7), including timezone, locale, dependency disappearance, hostile intake, emergency maintenance, and lifetime growth, remain mandatory where applicable.

`Languages and Data/1- stack and languages used.md` MUST explain final languages, frameworks, components, versions, responsibilities, licensing, platform suitability, decisions, and justified deviations. `Languages and Data/2- How the data is stored.md` MUST explain engine choices, actual formats, paths, schemas, ownership, independent lifecycle, backup, recovery, export, migration, and app-disappearance survival. Their first position in the reading order does not authorize selecting technology before requirements. Update affected technical records and the Master Plan together when intended behavior changes; preserve historical decisions as explicitly superseded evidence under [DEV-8](../1-%20Deciding%20a%20Project/109-%20Recording%20Questions%20and%20Decisions.md#dev-8).

For substantial persistent-data applications, this specification MUST separately assess the User Data Workspace, Master Plan, and Codebase architectures and their cross-references under [WORKSPACE-3](../4-%20Planning%20the%20Architecture%20and%20Data/401-%20Designing%20the%20Application%20Architecture.md#workspace-3). Folder planning, dual-JSON reconstruction, location selection, simulations, and deferred decisions belong in the current specification, not only in historical research.
