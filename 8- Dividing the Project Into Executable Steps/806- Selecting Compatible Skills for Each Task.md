### 33.2. SKILL-2 — Subordinate guidance and bounded skill execution

**Type:** ECOSYSTEM POLICY | **Origin:** USER POLICY | **Applies:** selection, invocation, and output of Agent Skills during development or application operations

The execution hierarchy is **(1) safety, privacy, legal constraints, and tool authorization → (2) Universal Rules constitution and owner-approved policies → (3) Approved project Master Plan and frozen acceptance criteria → (4) Exact phase/subphase execution contract (`EXEC-2`) → (5) Mandatory Ponytail operating behavior (`dietrichgebert/ponytail:skills/ponytail`) → (6) Mandatory Unlazy operating behavior (`leonxlnx/unlazy:.`) → (7) Approved applicable conditional or specialized skills (including conditional evaluation of `graphify-labs/graphify:graphify`) → (8) implementation choices**, subject to actual owner authority/approved exceptions under [GOV-3](../0-%20Start%20Here%20and%20Universal%20Rules/003-%20Rule%20Priority%20and%20Authority.md#gov-3). A skill MUST NOT override governing rules, owner policy, Master Plan, Phase Contract, project design/system contracts, security boundaries, or authorization. External installed skills MUST NOT silently become universal policy. A skill may guide use of existing authorized capabilities; it MUST NOT independently grant filesystem, deployment, credential, canonical-mutation, or production access, nor inherit every capability available to its host AI. Enforce only the permissions required by the workflow through [AUTH-1](../10-%20Building%20the%20Core%20and%20Backend/1007-%20Implementing%20Security%20and%20Authorization.md#auth-1) and [AI-2](../4-%20Planning%20the%20Architecture%20and%20Data/404-%20Defining%20APIs%20and%20Interfaces.md#ai-2). Frontend review normally needs no production DB writes, secrets, or backup deletion; documentation guidance normally needs no deployment authority.

**Deterministic task-start skill selection across all 18 stages (`0` through `17`):** Every task initialization MUST **(a)** select and apply **Ponytail** (`ALWAYS ON, 100% OF TASKS`) to govern implementation simplicity and efficiency (comprehend first, climb the 6-rung reuse/stdlib/minimalism ladder, fix root causes across all callers, avoid unrequested abstractions, and subordinate upstream comment/test/scope defaults to [DOC-1](../12-%20Connecting%20and%20Completing%20the%20Application/1203-%20Integrating%20Complete%20Features.md#doc-1), [EXEC-2](803-%20Dividing%20Subphases%20Into%20Atomic%20Tasks.md#exec-2), [TEST-1](../13-%20Testing%20Debugging%20and%20Repairing/1301-%20Preparing%20the%20Complete%20Test%20Environment.md#test-1), [NOFALL-1](../0-%20Start%20Here%20and%20Universal%20Rules/002-%20The%20Universal%20Constitution.md#nofall-1), and [NOFALL-2](../0-%20Start%20Here%20and%20Universal%20Rules/002-%20The%20Universal%20Constitution.md#nofall-2)); **(b)** select and apply **Unlazy** (`ALWAYS ON, 100% OF TASKS`) scaled proportionately by task mode (**Mode A** trivial in-context 5-point check without `GATES.md`/Depth Tree; **Mode B** standard `EXEC-2` acceptance verification; **Mode C** complex Depth Tree, acceptance gates, leaf/parent re-verification, and `EXEC-5` reconciliation; **Mode D** delegated/parallel disjoint `WRITE_SET` ownership and independent verification of returned subagent work), while keeping the raw `QUARANTINED` Unlazy package and `.mjs` scripts unexposed until cleared under [SKILL-3](../7-%20Attacking%20and%20Freezing%20the%20Master%20Plan/706-%20Checking%20Skills%20Dependencies%20and%20Conflicts.md#skill-3); and **(c)** automatically evaluate **Graphify** (`graphify-labs/graphify:graphify`) for structural/dependency applicability while strictly blocking its execution while `QUARANTINED`. Specialist skills (such as verified `PASS` engineering skills from `juliusbrussee/caveman` or `duolahypercho/andrej-karpathy-skills:skills/andrej-karpathy-skill`) are selected only when approved, useful, and compatible, and can never deselect Ponytail or Unlazy.

Skill output, including reviews, recommendations, plans, and classifications, MUST remain subject to evidence, relevant core validation/tests, project contracts, and applicable owner approval. Labels or successful skill execution do not establish factual truth or completion under [EVID-1](../0-%20Start%20Here%20and%20Universal%20Rules/009-%20Evidence%20and%20Truthful%20Completion.md#evid-1), [TEST-1](../13-%20Testing%20Debugging%20and%20Repairing/1301-%20Preparing%20the%20Complete%20Test%20Environment.md#test-1), and [DEV-13](../14-%20Reviewing%20the%20Entire%20Application/1408-%20Checking%20Rule%20and%20Requirement%20Compliance.md#dev-13). A skill reference MUST NOT replace project-specific phase objectives, files, constraints, target state, tests, evidence, or stopping conditions under [DEV-10](801-%20Creating%20Development%20Phases.md#dev-10). Materially guiding skill choices SHOULD be discoverable in phase/development metadata to support resumption; source/version/local modifications/permissions follow [SKILL-3](../7-%20Attacking%20and%20Freezing%20the%20Master%20Plan/706-%20Checking%20Skills%20Dependencies%20and%20Conflicts.md#skill-3). Neither a named adversarial skill nor this catalog supplies constitutional attack authority; verify actual owner authorization and the exact frozen candidate under [GOV-4](../17-%20Preserving%20Memory%20and%20Learning/1710-%20Improving%20the%20Universal%20Rules%20System.md#gov-4) and [section 42](../17-%20Preserving%20Memory%20and%20Learning/1710-%20Improving%20the%20Universal%20Rules%20System.md#s42).
