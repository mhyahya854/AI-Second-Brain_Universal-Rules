### 40.3. SIMPLE-1 — Simplicity and the identity-verified Ponytail role

**Type:** ECOSYSTEM POLICY | **Origin:** USER POLICY | **Applies:** code simplification and consideration of the owner's named Ponytail aid

Optimize for understandability, simplicity, appropriate reuse, minimal unnecessary abstraction, maintainability, clear responsibilities, and correctness. Necessary tests, error handling, comments, security checks, accessibility behavior, and documented contracts MUST NOT be removed merely to reduce line count. Simplification preserves [ARCH-3](../4-%20Planning%20the%20Architecture%20and%20Data/404-%20Defining%20APIs%20and%20Interfaces.md#arch-3), [TEST-1](../13-%20Testing%20Debugging%20and%20Repairing/1301-%20Preparing%20the%20Complete%20Test%20Environment.md#test-1), and the current phase's approved behavior.

The owner intends **Ponytail** to assist code simplicity and maintainability. [Section 44](../3-%20Deciding%20the%20Stack/310-%20Finalizing%20Technology%20Decisions.md#s44) identifies an actually inspected relevant upstream project and pinned revision; that evidence does not establish which later archive the owner will supply or authorize trusted execution. Before declaring or invoking its capabilities, verify the relevant Skill/project identity, source, revision, license, scope, and actual instructions; record the evidence and approved bounded role through [SKILL-3](../7-%20Attacking%20and%20Freezing%20the%20Master%20Plan/706-%20Checking%20Skills%20Dependencies%20and%20Conflicts.md#skill-3). Match the future supplied artifact to verified source; if that identity is unresolved, retain an explicit pending reference and do not substitute an unrelated similarly named project. General code-simplification work remains available under the verified contracts above. Ponytail MUST NOT be installed during this constitution-only mission. Later trusted use remains within the approved phase and cannot expand authority, erase safeguards, or change architecture by itself.
