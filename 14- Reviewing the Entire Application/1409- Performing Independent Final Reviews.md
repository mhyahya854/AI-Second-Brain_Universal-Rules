### 41.5. EXEC-5 — Verified checkmarks, Done Phases, and technical reviews

**Type:** ECOSYSTEM POLICY | **Origin:** USER POLICY | **Applies:** subphase/phase completion and application reviews

A phase/subphase heading MUST receive **✅ only after actual verification** of its applicable acceptance criteria. Example: `# ✅ SUBPHASE-001.003 — Database Initialization`. A checkmark is evidence-backed status, not decoration or prediction. Record changes, tests/checks actually performed, actual outcomes, errors, repairs, remaining issues, relevant tested commit/revision, and confirmation of passed acceptance criteria. Unrun checks are not passes; unknown results and incomplete work MUST NOT be marked done.

After all subphases and phase-level checks pass the [Three-Layer Conformance Gate](1408-%20Checking%20Rule%20and%20Requirement%20Compliance.md#dev-13)—Universal Rules, current App Master Plan, and current phase/subphase contract—move or record the parent phase in `Done Phases`, preserve original specifications and evidence, and update Project State. Verify actual implementation rather than the executor's confidence. Material regressions require correction or an explicit authorized disposition under [GOV-3](../0-%20Start%20Here%20and%20Universal%20Rules/003-%20Rule%20Priority%20and%20Authority.md#gov-3) and [DEV-13](1408-%20Checking%20Rule%20and%20Requirement%20Compliance.md#dev-13); an allowed exception MUST be visible and cannot present an unmet engineering guarantee as satisfied. Historical evidence MUST NOT be erased to make progress appear cleaner. A later discovered regression must be recorded with its affected revision and current status, without falsifying what an earlier check established.

Review records MUST be discoverable by applicable technical responsibility: frontend, backend, database, architecture, security, performance, integration, accessibility/rendered UI, recovery/migration, and final application review. Each review MUST state scope, revision, applicable specification, findings, severity, evidence, required repair, disposition, verification result, and remaining uncertainty. Proportional combined records are allowed where responsibilities remain explicit. Reviews supply findings and evidence; intended product behavior remains authoritative in the App Master Plan. Constitutional reviews additionally follow [GOV-5](../7-%20Attacking%20and%20Freezing%20the%20Master%20Plan/708-%20Performing%20Independent%20Adversarial%20Reviews.md#gov-5) and its integrated procedure.
