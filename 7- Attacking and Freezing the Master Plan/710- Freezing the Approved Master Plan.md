### 31.5. DEV-5 — Candidate-plan review and Plan Freeze Gate

**Type:** ECOSYSTEM POLICY | **Origin:** USER POLICY | **Applies:** application plans before implementation and material architectural replanning

Once the full application plan appears complete, freeze an identifiable candidate plan for hostile review before implementing it. Review MUST use the strongest reasonably available AI capability and relevant human/domain expertise and targeted technical research as appropriate to the plan and risk. Attack correctness, architecture, maintainability, longevity, data loss, storage/NAS failures, partitions, sync conflicts, migrations, offline behavior, platform differences, AI failures, security, privacy, performance, recovery, technology/dependency changes, small-model usability, developer replacement, and incomplete requirements where applicable.

Use **Attack → Fix → Attack → Fix**, preserving findings and dispositions, until no known material issue remains unresolved. The **Plan Freeze Gate** is: implementation may begin only when all known material architectural questions, contradictions, blocking risks, and unresolved design decisions have been resolved or explicitly accepted and documented by the applicable authority. Record the gated plan revision, evidence, accepted constraints, and any approved exceptions. Acceptance MUST NOT disguise a missing decision as an implementation task or waive a constitutional obligation without [GOV-3](../0-%20Start%20Here%20and%20Universal%20Rules/003-%20Rule%20Priority%20and%20Authority.md#gov-3) authority. The gate is an evidence-based decision, not mathematical “100% perfection.”

Authorized, bounded research experiments MAY precede Plan Freeze when needed to resolve a material planning question, including architecture, API/platform compatibility, performance, storage, migration, or crash/recovery behavior. For this gate and [DEV-2](../1-%20Deciding%20a%20Project/101-%20Understanding%20the%20User%27s%20Idea.md#dev-2), these are evidence-gathering activities, not adopted product implementation. Each experiment MUST define its research question or hypothesis, bound its scope to the evidence needed, and record results and relevant limitations through [DEV-6](../1-%20Deciding%20a%20Project/108-%20Selecting%20a%20Preliminary%20Project%20Blueprint.md#dev-6) and [DEV-8](../1-%20Deciding%20a%20Project/109-%20Recording%20Questions%20and%20Decisions.md#dev-8). Experimental code/data MUST be clearly identified as experimental/disposable or otherwise non-authoritative unless deliberately adopted later; the label does not authorize destruction of retained source data or required evidence. Small research scripts need no full production phase merely to gather evidence, but actual authorization, security/privacy controls, safe execution, and truthful evidence remain required.

Incorporation of experimental code or behavior into the product MUST pass the normal Plan Freeze, phase, conformance, security, testing, and evidence requirements. “Prototype” or “research” MUST NOT become a loophole for implementing substantial production scope before planning. Exploratory work MUST NOT silently become canonical architecture merely because it exists. Unresolved questions remain planning questions; this research route does not waive [DEV-11](../8-%20Dividing%20the%20Project%20Into%20Executable%20Steps/808-%20Creating%20Small%20Model%20Execution%20Packets.md#dev-11)'s stop-rather-than-guess rule or [DEV-13](../14-%20Reviewing%20the%20Entire%20Application/1408-%20Checking%20Rule%20and%20Requirement%20Compliance.md#dev-13)'s completion gate.

Material new architectural uncertainty during implementation returns the affected work to planning and this gate; do not silently improvise a different architecture. This application-plan review requirement does not itself authorize a constitutional attack; constitutional review follows the actual owner authorization and identified freeze under [GOV-4](../17-%20Preserving%20Memory%20and%20Learning/1710-%20Improving%20the%20Universal%20Rules%20System.md#gov-4), [GOV-5](708-%20Performing%20Independent%20Adversarial%20Reviews.md#gov-5), and [section 42](../17-%20Preserving%20Memory%20and%20Learning/1710-%20Improving%20the%20Universal%20Rules%20System.md#s42).

Necessary emergency maintenance may temporarily precede complete planning only under [DEV-17](../16-%20Maintaining%20Updating%20and%20Recovering/1603-%20Handling%20Incidents%20and%20Emergencies.md#dev-17), with bounded authority, evidence, and reconciliation before ordinary feature work resumes. This does not waive the gate for normal implementation.
